Kitchen Quest showcase

Every Kitchen Quest component in a demo state, for approval before any features are built. The plainer house style around the dark panels is what the profile picker and parent screens use. Everything is tappable, and nothing is saved.

Profile picker and theme switch

House style. Each profile has a chef's locker and picks its own theme, and the look switches when a profile is picked. These four are sample profiles. Pick Mom to see how safety banners read on an adult profile.

Lesson card

The quest card, with the gold test-kitchen seal on a lesson a parent has test-cooked. A lesson whose prerequisites aren't done yet shows as a locked "coming up" card instead of being hidden. The party-size picker starts at the queued serving count and changes the prep amounts and the cook steps. A baking lesson only offers the sizes its pan allows.

Prep: ingredients and gear

Ingredients fill an inventory grid, and equipment fills a gear loadout. Cook mode unlocks once every slot is filled. The knife and board use their category's icon, the fallback for tools without their own art.

Cook mode

The quest log, one step per screen, with the stage bar for progress within the lesson and the timer tray. Several timers can run at once, and each is labelled. Tap a ring to pause it. The egg step needs the thermometer check before Next unlocks.

Safety level

A banner across the top of the step, never a pill. On an adult profile it names only the hazard. It's information only; nothing waits for an adult.

Course progress, skills, and rank

The skill tree has one path per unit and one node per dish, at their real lengths. Clearing a quest lights its node. Skills earned show as crests. The rank shows as a level and title, and ranking up gets a full-screen level-up. Sample progress: 8 dishes cooked.

Photos: the trophy hall

Each photo hangs in a gold frame, and the gallery is the hall. These use the dish art as stand-ins for real photos.

Helper

The helper hasn't been specced yet. This is only its look: the guide on the floating Ask button (bottom right of this page) and a dialogue box styled to the theme.
