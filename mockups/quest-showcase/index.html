Theme showcase

Every component in both built themes, in a demo state. Both themes are approved. Pick a locker, or change a profile's theme, and every panel below switches. The plainer house style around the panels is what the profile picker and parent screens use. Everything is tappable, and nothing is saved.

Profile picker and theme switch

House style. Each profile has a chef's locker and picks its own theme, and the look switches when a profile is picked. These four are sample profiles: Ava and the shared profile use Kitchen Quest, and Leo and Mom use Chef's Passport. Switch any profile's theme on the right. Pick Mom to see how safety banners read on an adult profile.

Tutorial quest: the profile intro

What a profile sees right after picking its theme: one card per thing they'll do, each with a quick "try it". Short words, and nothing moves while a card is being read. Any card can be skipped, and the whole thing can be replayed from the profile's locker. Pick Mom above to see the adult versions: Cook this instead of Ask to cook, and Suggest a fix.

Kitchen Quest colours and type

The theme's tokens. Pixel type is for headings and labels only. Step text stays in the rounded body font at 21px, so it reads from arm's length.

Lesson card

The quest card, with the gold test-kitchen seal on a lesson a parent has test-cooked. A lesson whose prerequisites aren't done yet shows as a locked "coming up" card instead of being hidden. The party-size picker starts at the queued serving count and changes the prep amounts and the cook steps. A baking lesson only offers the sizes its pan allows.

Learn: the why, and where else it works

Before Prep, every lesson teaches the same four things: the move, why it works, the same move in other dishes, and what to do when it goes wrong. A lesson that reuses a skill opens with a callback to where the kid did it before. The "other dishes" card is built from the skill tags, so it lists cooked and not-yet-cooked lessons. The quiz is optional and never blocks cooking. In cook mode, steps with a Why? open the matching card.

Prep: ingredients and gear

Ingredients fill an inventory grid, and equipment fills a gear loadout. Cook mode unlocks once every slot is filled. The knife and board use their category's icon, the fallback for tools without their own art.

Cook mode

The quest log, one step per screen, with the stage bar for progress within the lesson and the timer tray. Several timers can run at once, and each is labelled. Tap a ring to pause it. The egg step needs the thermometer check before Next unlocks.

Safety level

A banner across the top of the step, never a pill. On an adult profile it names only the hazard. It's information only; nothing waits for an adult.

Course progress, skills, and rank

The skill tree has one path per unit and one node per dish, at their real lengths. Clearing a quest lights its node. Skills earned show as crests. The rank shows as a level and title, and ranking up gets a full-screen level-up. Sample progress: 8 dishes cooked.

Rating

One to five gold gems, never stars, then the reflection. Its last question is optional and asks the kid to carry the skill to another dish.

Photos: the trophy hall

Each photo hangs in a gold frame, and the gallery is the hall. These use the dish art as stand-ins for real photos.

Helper

The helper hasn't been specced yet. This is only its look: the guide on the floating Ask button (bottom right of this page) and a dialogue box styled to the theme.

Suggest a fix and the fix-request queue

"Suggest a fix" is on the lesson card and on every cook step. It asks for the parent PIN, unless an adult profile is cooking, then opens a short form in house style. The reporter says what it should say instead, so you can apply it without being the cook. Below is your owner queue. Applied fixes really change the lesson on this page, and Revert puts them back. One sample request fails the safety check on purpose.

Core art set

One generic icon per store section and per tool category, shared by both themes. Any ingredient or tool without its own icon falls back to these. Then the shakshuka ingredients and the dish art.

Parent screen: shopping list

House style, built for scanning on a phone in a store: grouped by store section with the section icons, big tap targets, no pills. This sample merges two queued lessons.
