# Unleavened swaps: the sorting pass

Claude's sort of every written lesson with leaven (2026-10-27, 59 of 153), by
what it would take for each to play during a family's unleavened days. The
design is in the plan, §6.20; this is the working list for phase 13. **The
easy swaps are written** (2026-10-28, build log 278): each lesson below lists
them as `unleavened` substitutions, soft unleavened bread first where it fits
(owner, 2026-10-28: "Use the soft bread where appropriate"), then matzah, then
gluten-free matzah. **The unleavened versions are written** (2026-10-29, build log
279): each lesson below that needs one has a variation marked `unleavenedDays`,
named after the dash.

New ingredients the swaps need: **matzah** and **matzah meal**, with the
label note "Plain matzah: flour and water only." (owner, 2026-10-27). For a
gluten-free family the swap is gluten-free too: corn tortillas where they
work, gluten-free matzah elsewhere (owner, 2026-10-27).

"L2" and so on marks a Level's own recipe; the rest are Cook Around the World
lessons.

## Can't be unleavened (15)

The leaven is the lesson. These don't play during the unleavened days.

- Challah, Pita bread, Skillet naan, Homemade pizza: yeast
- Dinner rolls (L3), Sandwich loaf (L3), Man'oushe (L3), Conchas (L3), Pulla (L3): yeast
- Japanese milk bread (L4), Chocolate babka (L4): yeast
- Buttermilk biscuits; Biscuits + sausage gravy: the rise is the skill
- Irish brown soda bread (L3): the soda is the bread
- Blueberry muffins: a muffin is its rise
