# Level recipe progression review

Copy of the working review doc ([Level recipe progression review](https://claude.ai/artifact/Pmx2xhA1HpZDsxfmckTnna)), saved 2026-10-24 after Unit 6. Its decisions aren't built yet; the plan and `content/levels/level-3.json` still show the old weeks.

## Purpose and ground rules

This review checks each Level week's recipe against two things: the skill the week teaches, and how hard the dish is for that point in the course. It works one unit at a time, starting with Level 3 Unit 1, and proposes a replacement wherever a dish should be dropped.

- **Fit:** the recipe should practise the week's actual skill, not just carry a matching tag.
- **Complexity across the year:** in general, dishes build toward more ingredients, more steps, and tighter timing over the year, so early weeks don't overwhelm a new cook and late weeks aren't trivial. A side dish or a slight step back is fine, and a dish that teaches its unit's skill very well stays even when it's simpler. The mastery week tests the unit's skill with less help. Bread units are exempt: they were moved early in every Level to stay clear of the Days of Unleavened Bread, so the year's climb is judged around them.
- **Looking ahead is the problem; looking back is fine.** Nobody taking a Level will have taken an earlier one, since they're all new (owner, 2026-10-24). Reusing a skill or recipe from an earlier Level, or from an earlier unit of the same Level, is good review and is preferred. A recipe that leans on a skill from a *later* unit is the thing to fix. Often the fix is a Level version of the dish (owner, 2026-10-24): a variation with slightly different steps, such as jarred minced garlic or pre-cut chicken. Lesson variations can already change, drop, or add ingredients and steps. Level versions show for every family, like any variation, as a less-prep choice (owner, 2026-10-24).
- **The same dish can be in two weeks** when it suits both and each week has other choices.
- **Skills learned in an earlier Level need a quick review.** Where a week relies on Level 2 to have taught a skill, a quick review is planned for it. These are written once Level 2 is written. They may link back to, or pull forward, the Level 2 lesson, drill, or knowledge page for a learner who's missing the skill.
- **Replacements come from the library first:** written lessons, then planned dishes, then wholly new recipes where nothing in the library fits. A new recipe is still checked against Cook Around the World for a home, as a group fill or a variation, before it's written (the plan's §12.4 rule).

The year builds in general, with no early unit overwhelming and no late unit trivial. Small dips (a side dish, a no-cook salad in the knife unit) are fine. Unit 2 (yeast dough) is exempt.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| 2 Yeast dough | 5–8 | 2–3½ hours, mostly rising | Several stages over a day or two; judging by feel (windowpane, proof, 190°F) | Exempt: bread units sit early to avoid the Days of Unleavened Bread |

## Patterns across units

- **Tags stand in for the week's skill.** In Unit 1, three of four weeks carry a dish-level skill (gentle eggs, stir-frying, binders) instead of the unit's, so options picked from those tags drift off-topic (Jollof rice, Swedish meatballs). Only week 2 carries `temping-meat`.
- **Chef's-knife work before Unit 3.** Owner, 2026-10-24: Level 2 gets basic knife safety for chopping, and Level 3 week 1 brings it in as a quick review. Unit 1's cuts are mostly plain chopping: onion and pepper (shakshuka, meatloaf, kofta), green onion rings, cilantro, avocado, smashed garlic (adobo). Three need more than a chop:
  - **Raw chicken in pieces** (fried rice, oyakodon): a raw-poultry cut that Unit 6 week 22 teaches. In Unit 1, a parent cuts the chicken (owner, 2026-10-24): the Level version marks that step adult-hands-on.
  - **Minced garlic** (shakshuka, fried rice): finer than a chop. The Level version uses jarred minced garlic.
  - **Thin slices** (smash burgers' onion and cucumber pickles, oyakodon's half-moons): a rough chop changes the dish. The Level versions make Smash burgers' onion and pickles optional and chop Oyakodon's onion.

- **Level 2 skills taken for granted.** Both units so far rely on Level 2 for something they don't teach again. Those are collected under Quick reviews from Level 2.

- **Searing a whole cut isn't taught in Level 2 or Level 3.** Level 2 sears only ground beef; whole cuts start in Level 4 Unit 2. Watch for it in later Level 3 weeks, such as week 34's beef stew.

- **Fit problems, not difficulty, drive most changes.** Most proposed changes are dishes that miss the week's skill (Jollof rice in week 3, Lomo saltado in week 15) or rely on a later skill (fish in week 9, whole-cut searing in weeks 10 and 16).
