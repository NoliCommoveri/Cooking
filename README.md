# Cooking

A cooking course app for kids: each lesson pairs one skill with one real dish,
then runs the cook with checklists, step-by-step cook mode, and timers.
Multi-family, on Cloudflare, at `cleanplate.immotus.app`. Working name: **Clean Plate Recipes**.

**Latest build log entry:** [197. 2026-10-25 — Level 3 Unit 3: knife cuts](docs/build-log.md#197-2026-10-25--level-3-unit-3-knife-cuts)

## Current features

The plan is in `cooking-class-app-PLAN.md`. Setup steps, all in a browser, are in `docs/setup.md`.

- **Accounts** (`src/worker/`, `public/`): open sign-up with an emailed code, an optional beta word, parent login, password reset by email, and a parent PIN for parent screens, which locks on its own when a parent leaves them.
- **Leaving** (Family settings, `src/worker/backup.js`): **Download our data** saves the family as one file; **Delete our family** (PIN and password) logs everyone out and hides the family at once, and a daily job erases it 30 days later.
- **Chef's lockers and profiles**: one person or several per profile, kid or adult, each with its own theme.
- **Kitchen**: each profile's own space, in Kitchen Quest or Chef's Passport, with three tabs pinned to the bottom: **Main quest / My journey** (this week and next open, the rest of its path under See more), **Side quests / More recipes** (every unit and recipe shelf, folded shut, with a search by dish or ingredient), and **Skill tree / Passport** (progress and rewards). Cards follow the family's diet settings, with swaps and left-out optional ingredients shown.
- **Kitchen intro**: Chef Ruth's cards, with screenshots, on the tabs, asking to cook, and each stage of a cook. Opens once for a profile with nothing cooked; **How it works** replays it.
- **Progress and rewards** (`public/rewards.js`, `src/content/rewards.js`): each dish lights its node on the skill tree or stamps the passport and its world map, and each skill earns a crest or pin. Six ranks, from Kitchen Apprentice to Executive Chef at 75 dishes; family recipes count toward rank only. Worked out from the cooks, never stored.
- **Celebrations**: after a cook, and on coming back to the kitchen, the new stamp or cleared quest, new skills, and a full-screen rank up, once per device.
- **Sound** (`public/sound.js`): made in the browser. A sizzle on Next, a stamp thunk, a skill chime, and a rank-up fanfare, with a mute toggle remembered on the device (timers always ring).
- **Lesson player** (`public/player.js`): each lesson in the profile's theme: the lesson card with the serving picker, Learn cards, Prep checklists, and Cook mode with safety banners, kitchen words, How much? (the amounts of the ingredients a step names), Why? cards, timers that ring until stopped, and the screen kept awake.
- **Suggest a fix** (`src/content/patch.js`, `src/worker/lessons.js`): parents and adult learners report a problem on a lesson or step with what it should say instead; the family sees what came of it on the lesson.
- **Fix requests** (owner screens): **Apply in the manager** opens the lesson in the manager section with the suggestion filled in; decline or reopen any of them, and revert an older lesson patch.
- **Voice step control** (`public/voice.js`): in cook mode, "next" (moves on and reads the step aloud), "back," and "again" (reads it again). Off until a parent turns it on in family settings; shown only where the browser can recognize speech.
- **Saved cooks** (`public/cooks.js`): each cook is saved to the family's account for every profile on it, with "Cooking with someone?" on the lesson card. A cook finished offline is sent when the connection returns. Lessons a profile has cooked say so in its kitchen. Nothing cooked on the Sabbath counts: it's saved, but the lesson stays up next and it isn't on Print our progress.
- **Queue** (`src/worker/plan.js`): the one "up next" list. Parents add lessons for one or more profiles, approve kids' requests, check each lesson's safety review, and mark lessons test-cooked. Kids ask with "I want to make this"; adults tap "Cook this". A kid starting a lesson that isn't on their list needs the parent PIN, approved as an extra meal or in place of an assigned one, so assigned lessons never get pushed back. A course lesson approved early leaves its later place, so it never shows twice. Each queued lesson is one line that opens to its tools. **Preview as a learner** (there, in Add a lesson, in Our recipes, and in the course editor) goes through a lesson as one of the family's profiles would see it, with nothing saved or counted. A Course / Additional toggle switches between the course and lessons assigned on top of it; + Add a lesson opens a dialog.
- **Courses and the weekly schedule** (`src/worker/schedule.js`): set a course up once for one or more profiles, and each week's lessons load into the queue. Weeks run Sunday to Friday (Saturday is the Sabbath); each week's lessons join the queue on its Sunday. On the Sabbath a "Shabbat shalom" banner shows and due dates are hidden. Unfinished lessons roll to next week; Skip this recipe, Cook another recipe, and Skip this week, each with Undo. The course card shows This week open and Next week folded, with the week's changes in a "⋯" menu. The course editor folds its lessons by path.
- **Shopping** (`src/content/shop.js`, `src/worker/shopping.js`), optional: **What you'll need** shows the ingredients for the coming lessons week by week. A parent can make a shopping list for the next few uncooked lessons, tick what's on hand, and check the rest off in the store; it's merged, rounded to store packages, and grouped by aisle. Three views: Upcoming (the default), Lists (newest first), and Staples.
- **Kitchen staples** (Shopping → Staples): added by searching for an ingredient; grouped first as "May already have" in Upcoming and before making a list; on the list itself, in their aisles.
- **Casual and homeschool modes**: homeschool adds due days for kids and overdue for parents; casual can turn on Plan our weeks.
- **Reflect** (`public/player.js`): after Plate it, each profile on the cook rates it (gems or chef's hats), says what they'd change, and can name another dish that uses the move; plus an optional rating from the eaters. Saved even if the connection drops.
- **Grades** (homeschool mode, `src/worker/grades.js`): parents grade each cook with the 1–4 rubric, a grade in their own words, or both, from the Grades page or Progress. Kids never see grades; nothing is graded on the Sabbath.
- **Transcript** (homeschool mode): one profile's printable course record with the homeschool's name, course, school year, dates, every counted cook with hours and grade, skills, and the parent's final grade and credit.
- **Print our progress** (Parents → Progress): what each profile has cooked, with date, dish, cuisine, skill, and rating, printable or as a CSV. **Didn't make it** on a row takes back a cook a kid marked but didn't make, with Undo.
- **Photos switch** (`/ops`): photos are off for every family until the owner turns them on for one.
- **Chef Ruth** (`public/ruth.js`, `src/worker/setup.js`): the helper, a scripted chat with a floating button on the parent screens. Her **Getting started** runs on its own for a new family and can't be skipped: family name and PIN, servings, diet, learners, kid logins, casual or homeschool, then a course or a first lesson. It can be run again from her button to change answers, until there's a lesson in the queue or a dish cooked. Her **Show me around** tours every parent screen, starting with the Queue: a look at each, then how to use it, and Let me see it again for a longer look. She types like a person: typing dots, then one message at a time.
- **Test-cook notice**: parents from before Getting started see a one-time note, above the lockers, to read a lesson through before their kids cook it; Getting started ends with the same reminder.
- **Demo page** (`/demo`): no account needed. Two tracks: Cook Around the World (the sample lessons in either theme, diet and shopping previews, the course by dish or skill) and Structured Progression (a Level 3 week and its part pages, the four Levels, and a learner's progress).
- **Syllabus** (`/syllabus`): Cook Around the World as a homeschool course, with objectives, units, hours, pacing, a suggested credit, and the rubric. Printable.
- **Structured Progression syllabus** (`/syllabus/structured-progression/`): the four Levels, how a week works, Level 3's objectives, units, every week's parts, hours and credit, pacing, safety, and assessment; Levels 1, 2, and 4 as outlines. Printable.
- **Course outline** (`content/course.json`): all 98 planned dishes (59 numbers, with pick-one choices) in teaching order, with their skills and prerequisites, checked by the linter.
- **Lesson numbers** (`src/content/course.js`): come from the course order; pick-one choices share a number (1a, 1b, 1c), 59 in all.
- **Appointed Times unit** (unit 8 in `content/units.json`): lessons beside the numbered course, labeled A1, A2: unleavened flatbread and cottage cheese crescent rolls so far.
- **Family recipes** (Parents → Recipes, `src/content/recipe.js`, `src/worker/recipes.js`): a parent adds the family's own recipes one thing at a time (basics, ingredients from the list or typed in, tools, steps with safety levels and timers, meal type, a "why we make this" card, then review). The same unclean-food and food-safety checks as lessons; a wash-hands step is added after raw food. They show on an "Our recipes" shelf in every kitchen and can be queued, shopped for, and cooked like lessons. Only that family sees them.
- **Sharing recipes** (`src/worker/sharing.js`): a parent shares a recipe as General or Feast day. One from our ingredient list goes straight into **User-shared recipes**, with no family named; a typed-in ingredient waits for the owner's review; a feast-day recipe flagged for leaven gets the owner's notice with Send for review and Share recipe. Any family can search the directory, add a recipe to its own, and report one.
- **Owner: Recipe reviews and Reported recipes**: clear or decline a review (deciding the Unleavened badge), and hide or unhide any shared recipe.
- **Swap for an Appointed Time meal instead** (`src/worker/swap.js`): under a queued lesson's tools, parents only. February 1 to May 15 it offers only unleavened meals (the unit's lessons, other lessons, then recipes); the rest of the year, the unit, feast-day course lessons, and every feast-day recipe. The course lesson it replaces goes first next week and never counts as behind; a hand-queued one comes off. Undo swap puts it back.
- **Unleavened badge** (`src/content/leaven.js`, `content/rules/leaven.json`): on every lesson whose ingredients and steps have no leaven.
- **Good for Feast Days badge**: on Appointed Times lessons, course lessons the owner ticks on the lesson's dish card in the editor, and recipes shared as feast-day. Both badges show on kitchen cards, dish cards, Swap, My recipes, and the directory.
- **Parent menu**: every parent screen behind one Menu button, in groups: Learning path, Recipes, Setup, and Owner (the owner only).
- **Dish cards**: on the queue and course screens, a dish's name opens a card with a line about it, its badges, its main ingredients, how it cooks, and the time.
- **Kid logins** (`src/worker/kidlogin.js`): a parent gives a kid profile a login (first name and last initial, one of 30 cooking pictures, a password). That kid's device opens only their kitchen; the server refuses everything else. A kid login lasts 14 days, renewed each time the app is opened.
- **Parent screens**: profiles, family name, default servings, diet filters, voice step control, other parent logins, and the PIN. Each list's add button is at its top right, and adding opens a dialog (a course or recipe opens its own page); long sections fold shut.
- **Owner screens**: the beta word, the family list, setting a parent's password, fix requests, recipe reviews, and reported recipes.
- **Ops page** (`/ops`): migrations (one schema file today; Re-apply draft, Restore draft tables, and Finalize when a build has a draft migration), Releases (numbered releases, each launched in order for everyone, with a preview per family and Undo launch of the latest; `src/worker/release.js`), the owner flag, whether GitHub is connected for the manager section, backup download and restore, clear everything, remove or restore one family, and Bring back a family its parents deleted, within its 30 days.
- **Manager section** (`/manage`, `src/worker/manage.js`, `public/manage/`): the owner's own sign-in. Add, rename, reorder, and remove units; add lessons (blank or a copy), move, hide, and unhide them; edit every part of a lesson in a form built from the lesson schema; edit ingredients, tools, skills, kitchen words, cuisines, tastes, and the food rules; tag every dish on the Tags page; upload dish pictures. Checked as you type by the content linter. **Preview as a learner** runs a lesson in either theme, unsaved changes included, with no checklist to tick and nothing saved.
- **Saving content** (`src/worker/github.js`, `.github/workflows/content-publish.yml`): a save is checked again and committed to the `content-edits` branch; GitHub builds, lints, and tests it and puts it on `main`, which deploys. **Saves** shows each one's status, with Undo; a lesson's History loads any earlier version.
- **Hidden lessons** (`content/hidden.json`): out of the library, the course numbers, and every family's course plan until unhidden.
- **Theme picker mockup** (`mockups/theme-picker.html`): four candidate themes with a sample lesson, so the kids can pick one.
- **Dish illustrations** (`content/images/dishes/`): one picture for each planned dish.
- **Theme showcase** (`mockups/quest-showcase/`): every component in both approved themes, Kitchen Quest and Chef's Passport.
- **Lesson content** (`content/`): 37 lessons (all 12 of Unit 1, all 20 of Unit 2, 3 more samples, and the 2 Appointed Times lessons), and the ingredient, tool, skill (60), and cuisine lists, as JSON.
- **Level 3 content** (release 1, `content/levels/level-3.json`): Level 3 Cook's 9 units and 36 weeks, each with its skill, recipe, mastery check, and drill, knowledge, and Beyond the Recipe page, plus 21 Level-only recipes and Units 1 to 3's written recipes and part pages; edited on the manager's Level 3 page.
- **Level courses** (release 1, `src/worker/plan.js`): setting up a course asks Which program?, Cook Around the World or Level 3 Cook; a Level course runs one week a week, its recipes in week order, and the course card shows "Week 5 of 36".
- **Level 3 recipes in Side quests** (release 1): the Level's own written recipes (labels L1, L2…) in their own unit in the kitchen, cookable and queueable like any lesson.
- **Optional Level parts** (release 1): a Level week is Knowledge, Drill, Recipe, Beyond the Recipe; any but the recipe can be turned off at setup or later, with the planned hours shown; any part can be skipped.
- **Level progress** (release 1): a Level learner's own progress tab (the weeks bar, hours toward ~125, units as chapters or passport pages with a crest or visa stamp, and My cooking collection), level-ups for units mastered, and the completion certificate; the Level's weeks and part rows on Progress, its CSV, and the transcript.
- **Best-fit Level recipes** (release 1, `src/worker/picks.js`): a new Level course keeps each week's best fit from its options for the people it's for; the parent changes a week in the course editor or with Swap recipe.
- **The kitchen's Level week** (release 1): this week's recipe and its drill, knowledge, and Beyond the Recipe pages, each checked off with Done (not on the Sabbath); parts still open from earlier weeks; the parent's Take back; and a mastery week's "cook it on your own" and "Why did it work?".
- **Content linter** (`tools/lint-content.js`): checks every lesson against the food rules, including the unclean-food block, the food-safety steps, and scaling. Runs on every push, on every save from the manager section, and in the browser as the owner edits.
- **Our tastes** (release 1, `src/worker/tastes.js`, `public/tastes.js`): each profile rates proteins, flavors, and key ingredients from 1 to 5 stars (one absolute favorite per list) or Haven't tried, and the family sets its time, budget, tools, and how adventurous. Also in Getting started and as Chef Ruth's "Ask what we like to eat".
- **Variations** (`src/content/variations.js`, `src/worker/variations.js`): complete versions of a lesson with a preference built in: 9 so far, on 6 lessons (another protein, beans or tofu, or more heat). Edited in the manager section and checked as whole lessons; with the next release, a parent picks the version, and the player and shopping lists use it.
- **Recipe gaps** (owner screens, `src/worker/gaps.js`): which pick-one groups leave families with nothing that fits and why, counts only; Write next opens the variation or dish to write in the manager with its tags filled in; a coverage table and what people cook.
- **Picks** (release 1, `src/content/score.js`, `src/worker/picks.js`): each version and dish is scored for the people cooking it from their stars and the family's limits. The best version plays unless a parent picks one, a new course keeps the best dish in each pick-one group, Add a lesson starts with Best for them, Swap lists the best fit first, and Chef Ruth answers "What should we cook?". An optional ingredient someone hates is left out. Reflect ratings teach each profile's tastes over time, cuisines included. Nothing is dropped: a 1 star or a limit only ranks a dish lower.
- **Tastes and tags** (`content/tastes.json`, `src/content/tastes.js`): the proteins, flavors (with example dishes), and key ingredients families will rate, and every dish's protein, flavors, heat, budget, familiarity, and vegetables tags. Not shown to families yet.
- **Content file layout** (`tools/format-content.js`): every content file in the one layout the manager section writes.
- **Scaling** (`src/content/`): amounts and step counts change with the serving size, shown in measuring-cup and spoon amounts.
- **Lesson preview** (`preview/lesson-preview.html`): each written lesson, read-only, at every serving size. Tap an underlined kitchen word to see what it means.
- **Kitchen words** (`content/terms.json`): what cooking words like dice, mince, and simmer mean, in kid terms. The linter won't let a lesson use one that isn't defined. They're tappable in Cook mode and on a Level's part pages.
