# content/levels/

The Levels (plan §12): Level 2 Apprentice (`sp-2.json`), Level 3 Cook
(`sp-3.json`), and Level 4 Chef (`sp-4.json`), each with its units, weeks,
part pages, and recipe cards; `recipes/` holds the Level-only recipes as
they're written. They're release 1 (plan §8.6): nothing in this folder is
shown to a family until release 1 is launched on `/ops`, or the family's
preview is on.

Each Level's `sideUnit.prefix` is its letter: B (Level 2), C (Level 3), D
(Level 4). A is kept for Level 1, and S and U are Cook Around the World's
and Appointed Times'. A dish's label is the letter, its week, and a for
Familiar or b for Explore: C4a.
